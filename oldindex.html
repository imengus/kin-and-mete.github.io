Our Statement

We are two medical students, from the University of Bristol and UCL, and we created this service to offer personalised, specialised preparation for UK medical school interviews.

Each student receives tailored sessions that go beyond what general guides can offer. We explore interview topics in real depth—from medical ethics and the NHS to communication skills and motivation for medicine. Our aim is for students to walk into their interviews understanding the material, rather than reciting it.

We value the relationships we build with our students, and our support does not end on interview day. As we progress through our own training, we hope to stay in touch—offering mentorship, guidance through medical school, and advice on specialty portfolios and research publications. We are here for the long term.

Our Experience

I scored 3000 in my UCAT (Band 2, top 5%) and received offers from UCL and Southampton (rejected King's before they gave me an offer). Through time-optimisation and structured prep, I achieved 11 9s at GCSE and A*A*A*A*A* at A-Level.

Realising how alive I felt in teaching, I expanded into mentoring students through med-school interviews.

Over 15+ months travelling in Middle East/ South Asia, I communicated with thousands of people, from tribesmen to politicians. Thanks to the life-experience and intuition I gained, I give cutting feedback on areas teachers and friends may have missed. Together, we will build custom resources and plans for all areas of your preparation. Let's collaborate in your success!

My sessions focus on understanding over memorisation—encouraging students to think out loud to build clear, structured reasoning. Above all, I will ensure every student feel confident and well-prepared. If you are looking for a tutor invested in your success, I would love to hear from you.
